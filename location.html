Getting here

However you're travelling

✈️

From the airport

Take the Airtrain to Roma Street or Central Station, then walk or catch a short taxi up the hill. By car it's around 20 minutes.

🚆

By train

Roma Street Station is around a 10 minute walk away, through Roma Street Parkland.

🚌

By bus

The free city loop bus stops right outside the hotel, linking you to the CBD.

🚗

By car

On-site parking is available, subject to availability. Let us know when you book.

Things to do nearby

Roma Street Parkland

Lush gardens, walking paths and picnic spots, just down the road.

Queen Street Mall

Brisbane's main shopping street, with hundreds of shops, cafés and restaurants.

Suncorp Stadium

Catch rugby league, rugby union, football and concerts at Brisbane's iconic stadium.

South Bank

The city's beach-style lagoon, riverside dining and the Queensland Museum and galleries.

Fortitude Valley

Bars, live music and some of the city's best places to eat.

Spring Hill heritage walk

Wander streets of historic workers' cottages and the old Spring Hill Baths.
