Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you arrive. Can't find your answer? Call us any time.

Your stay

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 10:00 am. Reception is open 24 hours, so late arrivals are welcome. Ask us about early check-in or late check-out; we'll do our best depending on availability.

Is reception open all night?

Yes. Our front desk is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Can I store my luggage?

Yes. Leave your bags with reception before check-in or after check-out and enjoy the rest of your day in Brisbane.

Do you have rooms with a balcony?

Yes, selected rooms have a private balcony. Choose the Queen with Balcony when booking, or call us to request one.

Is there a lift?

Yes, there is lift access to all guest floors.

Getting here & parking

Is parking available?

Yes, we have on-site parking. Spaces are limited and offered subject to availability, so please mention it when you book.

How do I get to the city?

The free city loop bus stops right outside the hotel. You can also walk to the CBD through Roma Street Parkland in around 15 minutes.

How do I get here from the airport?

Take the Airtrain to Roma Street or Central Station, then walk or take a short taxi ride up the hill. By car or taxi it is around 20 minutes, depending on traffic. See our location page for details.

Booking

Why should I book direct?

Booking with us directly gets you our best available rate, and you can talk to our own team about changes and special requests.

Do you offer group or corporate rates?

Yes. Contact us with your dates and number of rooms and we will send you a quote.

How do I change or cancel my booking?

Call us on (07) 3831 6000 or email stay@metropolitanspringhill.com.au. Cancellation terms depend on the rate you booked.
