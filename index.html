Welcome

Your easy base in Brisbane

The Metropolitan sits on Leichhardt Street in Spring Hill, one of Brisbane's oldest and most charming inner suburbs. You're close enough to walk into the CBD, but far enough from the noise to get a proper night's sleep.

Whether you're in town for business, a game at Suncorp Stadium, a hospital appointment or a weekend away, our friendly team is at the front desk around the clock to help you settle in.

  • Every room air-conditioned with a private ensuite
  • Lift access to all floors
  • Rooftop terrace with city views
  • Free city loop bus stops at the door
